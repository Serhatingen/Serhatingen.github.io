Terms of Use

Effective: 30 October 2026

Personal integration

YouTube Shorts Automation is an owner-operated tool for moving prepared videos and metadata from a designated Drive queue to an authorized YouTube channel. It is not a public upload service, offers no visitor accounts and is not affiliated with or endorsed by Google or YouTube.

Authorized content and access

The operator is responsible for maintaining appropriate rights and permissions to upload each video, its audio and associated metadata, and for complying with applicable law, YouTube policies and the YouTube Terms of Service. Access must be limited to accounts and folders the operator is authorized to use.

Publication and operational limits

The operator controls the queue, metadata, channel authorization and privacy/publication settings. The workflow uses upload history and resumable-session checks to reduce duplicate uploads. Service outages, quota limits, revoked access and expired sessions can interrupt execution. These checks do not guarantee uninterrupted execution, publication timing, views or the absence of duplicates under all conditions. Ambiguous uploads require reconciliation before a new insertion.

Data and discontinuation

The Privacy Policy describes data access and storage. The operator can stop using the tool and revoke Google access at any time. Stopping the workflow does not automatically delete videos already uploaded or retained records.
