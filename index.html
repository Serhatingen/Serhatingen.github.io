YouTube Shorts Automation

An owner-operated tool that uploads prepared videos from a designated Google Drive queue to the owner's authorized YouTube channel.

Purpose

The operator prepares video files and matching JSON metadata, then runs a scheduled uploader. The current daily workflow selects at most one new upload per Istanbul calendar day unless the operator explicitly overrides that limit.

How the workflow uses Google services

  1. A Google Drive service account accesses the folders explicitly shared with it and reads video files, metadata and upload history.
  2. The channel owner grants YouTube upload and read-only permissions through Google's OAuth consent flow.
  3. The uploader verifies the channel, reads recent upload identifiers and source markers, uploads the selected video and records its result.

This is a personal integration for the operator's own channel. This website provides information only: it has no Google sign-in form, does not accept credentials and does not offer account access to visitors.
