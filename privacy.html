Privacy Policy

Effective: 30 October 2026 · Applies to the current owner-operated daily upload workflow.

Operator and purpose

Serhatingen operates this personal integration to publish prepared videos to the operator's own YouTube channel. This informational website has no registration or account-connection feature.

Information accessed

The uploader accesses videos and JSON metadata in designated Google Drive folders, including file IDs, filenames and file properties. It reads and updates upload-history files. Through the authorized YouTube account it reads channel details, upload-playlist entries, video IDs and tags to verify the destination and reconcile earlier uploads. It sends the selected video, title, description, tags, localization and publication settings to YouTube.

Permissions and storage

Google Drive access uses a service account authorized by folder sharing. YouTube access uses the scopes youtube.upload and youtube.readonly. OAuth client credentials, a refresh token and service-account credentials are configured in the operator's private GitHub Actions secrets. Upload history, video and metadata fingerprints, video IDs and resumable-session information are retained in the operator-controlled queue state. Temporary downloaded videos are processed on the execution runner. Operational logs may include filenames, metadata and upload results.

Use, transfer and retention

Google user data is used for the upload workflow and recovery from errors. It is not sold or used by this application for advertising or general-purpose AI model training. The workflow transfers videos and metadata from Google Drive through its execution environment to YouTube. GitHub provides workflow execution, secret storage and logs; Google provides Drive, OAuth and YouTube services. Queue history remains until removed by the operator; logs follow the repository's retention settings. Published videos follow their YouTube settings until changed or deleted by the owner.

The application's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. YouTube functionality is also subject to the YouTube Terms of Service and Google Privacy Policy.

Control and deletion

The operator can stop scheduled workflows, revoke the application's access at Google Account permissions, and remove the service account from Drive folder sharing. Revocation stops future authorized access but does not itself remove stored queue history, logs or videos. These must be deleted through Drive, GitHub and YouTube respectively. Deleting upload history requires reconciliation first to avoid duplicate uploads.

This website and contact

The static pages contain no application-installed analytics, advertising scripts, forms or tracking cookies. The hosting provider may process request and connection information under its own policies. For questions or deletion requests, use the contact options on the operator's GitHub profile. Do not include credentials or private video information in public messages.
